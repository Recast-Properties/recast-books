## Rules learned today - keep them

  share the words). Never reword it, never offer free text in its place.
- **A card can hold several bank lines.** Anything that acts on "the card" must act per line - the first
  `placeholderEntry_` would have lumped three charges into one 337.07 that no receipt could match. Found before Paul
  saved, by reading the real envelope. Read what code really made before telling him to click.
- **Before asking Paul anything about his money, grep `docs/decisions.md`, the handoffs and the CHANGELOG** for the
  amount and the word. A handoff line is a summary; the decision holds his words.
- **Reading the live workbook:** gviz through Claude in Chrome with `tqx=out:html` and `sheet=<tab>&headers=1`.
  **`out:csv` makes Chrome download a file** - it left `data (3).csv` in Paul's Downloads (told to him). The
  `books-cache` copy of the Feed tab is refreshed only by a matcher run, never by the workbook's own Save - do not
  trust it for the Inbox's state.

- **Checking a card without the workbook:** a copy of `Inbox.html` with `Style.html` inlined and
  `google.script.run` stubbed, served from the scratchpad on 127.0.0.1, driven in the in-app browser.

  deploy. `lib/`, the prompts, the functions: the site's deploy. A new gate reason needs its words in `Inbox.html`
