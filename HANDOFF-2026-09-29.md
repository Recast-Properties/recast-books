## Rules learned today - keep them

- **The words `NEED RECEIPT FROM` are how the books find a placeholder** (`NEED_RECEIPT`, `lib/gate.mjs`; the Inbox
  and a lint share them). Never reword the description; never offer a free-text alternative.
- A placeholder is voided on its own date (the bank balance is right every day); an earlier copy is voided today (the
  ingest's rule). Both go before the post; a Save that died in between is clicked again and only posts.
- A change to `Inbox.html` or `Menu.gs` alone needs a push; `Code.gs` or `lib.gs` (any `lib/*.mjs` the writer
  bundles - `gate.mjs` today) needs the deploy too. The site needs its own deploy for `lib/`, the prompt and the
  functions. A new gate reason needs its words in THREE places: `Inbox.html` `flagText_`, the poller's `gateText_`
  (both poller projects pushed), and the lint.
- **Checking a card without the workbook:** build a copy of `Inbox.html` with `Style.html` inlined and
  `google.script.run` stubbed (a Proxy answering from a table of made-up envelopes), serve the scratchpad on
  127.0.0.1 and drive it in the in-app browser - the script runs, the Save payloads can be read back. A `file://`
  page outside the project renders as a static picture; it has to be served.
- Deploys run from the session on Paul's word "deploy" (writer `clasp deploy -i`, site `npm run deploy`); `clasp
  login` is his step when it expires.
