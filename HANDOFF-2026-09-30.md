## Rules learned - keep them

- **A check with no name is booked from the bank's picture of it, never from memory** - Paul named two other
  purposes before the picture (memory `check-image-before-booking-a-check`). Numbers that tie to the cent confirm a
  recollection; anything else needs the document. Build the helper after the evidence, not before.
- **Plain references into the Journal drift** when its rows are deleted; INDIRECT text does not. Whole columns do
  not help when the helper lives on another tab.
- **A Sheets write that times out** ("Service Spreadsheets failed while accessing document") is the formulas being
  too heavy to compute on write - SUMIFS, not SUMPRODUCT, over 20,000 rows.
- `Inbox.html` / `Menu.gs` / `oneOffScripts.gs` alone: a push. `Code.gs` / `lib.gs` (any `lib/*.mjs` the writer
  bundles - `scripts/build-gs.mjs` MODULES): push AND deploy. `lib/`, the prompts, the functions: the site's deploy.
- Reading the live workbook: gviz through Claude in Chrome with `tqx=out:html` and `sheet=<tab>&headers=1` (or
  `range=`); the sheet's own UI (Name box, a cell's error tooltip) when a value is an error - gviz shows only #N/A.
- Match statement lines asks which bank only when two banks have lines waiting.
- **Fold new things into what exists** (D-063): a check goes on a tab that refreshes itself or in the 3 AM email, never
  a new menu item or page for Paul to remember.
- **A tab label must not start with "="** - `setValues` makes it a formula (#ERROR).
- **The books-cache `tab/Feed` can be a day old** - read the Feed live (gviz) before counting cards.
- **Approving a bank card can replay a purchase the old books hold under different cents** (09-03 Brushwood) - the
  box shows it as a books line with no bank line; check the old rows before approving a card for an old date.
- **Read the tab Paul is looking at before evaluating it** - "Report - Balance sheet" was a 09-11 test snapshot; the
  fresh report was different. A tab written only by a menu run goes stale; a timer or a write-path refresh does not.
- **A time-driven trigger's handler must be listed in the D-056 lint's entry points** (`refreshBalanceSheetHourly`).
- **Before calling a scheduled job broken, check when it is scheduled** - the API-costs job had simply never had a turn.
