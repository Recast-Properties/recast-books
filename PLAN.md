## The business, briefly

Recast Properties LLC. DFW / Ellis County, Texas. Foreclosure-auction acquisitions,
rehab, resale. Paul operates; Dennis Little takes 50% of each project and also makes
interest-bearing advances to the LLC. Six properties on book. Roughly $2M of resale
volume. One Google Sheets workbook holds everything.

## The problem in one line

There is no source of truth — every tab is a view with nothing underneath it, which is
why the same contractor appears on four tabs and totals nowhere.

## 4 · Chart of accounts

See `docs/chart-of-accounts.md`. Series encodes treatment: 1000s/1400s balance sheet,

**The release rule** is the load-bearing mechanic: on the settlement date in the
property registry, a property's whole accumulated 1000s balance moves to 5000. The
first draft of this plan had no revenue accounts and no release mechanic — two
reviewers caught it independently.

## 7 · Bank reconciliation

Paul uploads statements to a `statements@` channel through the existing poller / Blobs /
queue path (D-004). The agent stages lines, auto-matches on date + amount + payee, and
proposes near-misses.

**Three clearing sources, not one:** a bank/card statement line; an ALTA settlement line
stamped `ALTA <closing date>`; or a member-capital settlement where `paid_by` is not
Recast. Ashburne's $46,500 commission, $15,500 closing costs and $19,000 concession were
netted at settlement and never touched a bank account — under a bank-only rule they sit
in the exception list forever and the period never closes.

## 8 · Member equity

Separate Dennis's two hats — advances with interest are debt (2010/2000/9030), profit
share is equity (9000/9010). Capital account per member. Every Paul-paid / Dennis-paid
row must resolve to a reimbursement or a capital contribution. Draws are not expenses:
the $176,141.44 drawn against Ashburne belongs in the 9000s and must never reduce
project profit. Interest on member loans needs a written note with a stated rate.

## 9 · Monthly close

**Second Friday** — clear of the first-Tuesday courthouse auction and of month-end
closings. A close scheduled for auction week is a close that lapses by month three.

Open Items tab with a release valve: the period closes if unresolved items total under
$250 or number fewer than five. Full close with both exception lists genuinely empty
runs quarterly.

Don't found a new ritual — extend the 3am digest that already runs into a monthly
variant that pre-computes the checklist. Close becomes reading one email.

**Quarterly:** send the accountant the trial balance. It is the only genuine
second-party review available (D-005).

## 10 · The accountant's packet

P&L and balance sheet · trial balance mapped to return lines · per-property job cost
with settlement statement · year-end inventory of properties held at cost · 1099
worksheet with W-9 status · fixed asset and depreciation schedule · mileage log if
elected · member capital accounts and loan balances · reconciliation report per account
per period.

## 11 · Phased migration

Nothing writes to the live workbook until parallel-run proves out.

### Now — W-9s, no dependencies

Text a fillable W-9 to all nine over-threshold payees plus the five just below. Capture
missing surnames while on the phone. Get the classification read on the four labor-only
payees first. Add the amount ceiling to the live autofile gate — one comparison in code
already deployed.

*Why this jumps the queue: it is the only item with real money and a statutory deadline,
and it depends on nothing below.*

### Phase 0 — Freeze and snapshot
Dated copy stored outside the workbook. Record every block total and property summary as
the baseline. Delete the stale `RECAST BIZ backup 2026-08-25 1350` tab.

### Phase 0.5 — Opening trial balance
Bank and card balances per account, capitalized basis per property held, member loan
principal, escrow receivable. Nothing ties to anything without an opening position.

### Phase 1 — Build the shell
New workbook, structure only, no data, no automation pointed at it. *(Blocked on Q-2,
Q-3.)*

### Phase 2 — Migrate history, faithfully then corrected
Two steps, two gates, because a migration that corrects while it copies can never prove
it copied correctly.

- **2a Faithful copy.** Parse every property tab, every RECAST BIZ block, *and the
  cash-advance/draw tab*. No corrections. Dry run first.
  **Gate:** every property's total project cost and net profit, and all eleven RECAST
  BIZ block totals, match the Phase 0 baseline to the cent.
- **2b Corrections, each logged.** 420 Alyssa's interest moves. Rehab items leave
  overhead Materials. The `Business` catch-all maps to real accounts.
  **Gate:** every correction is its own dated row with a reason, and the sum of
  corrections explains the entire difference from baseline.

### Phase 3 — Normalize vendors, build the monitor
Collapse aliases around the W-9s already returning. Threshold in a named cell
($2,000 / $600). Wire the payment-side block into the writer. TIN matching before filing.
**Gate:** payments by canonical vendor equal the sum of GL rows. *This proves arithmetic,
not correctness — two different payees merged in error passes it. Spot-check by hand.*

### Phase 4 — Reconcile the year to date
Build the `statements@` channel and matching layer. Import every statement for the
current year. Work both exception lists to empty.

### Phase 5 — Repoint the automation, run in parallel
Writer appends single GL rows instead of locating blocks — the `BLOCKED` failure mode
disappears. Agent-side property routing (per-property inboxes cut as unnecessary
ceremony). Writer files to **both** workbooks for two weeks.
**Gate:** fourteen consecutive days of zero-variance diffs, comparing row count, dollar
total, and per-account and per-property distribution — not just totals, since two
offsetting misclassifications net to zero. A *classification* difference is logged for
review rather than resetting the clock, since the new writer is meant to classify better.

### Phase 6 — Cutover
Old workbook read-only, permanent archive, never deleted. Automation writes to the new
workbook only. Monthly close begins.

### Guardrails, all phases
No script ever writes to the old workbook. Every script dry-runs before applying and
backs up before writing. Totals verified twice by different paths. A migration that
cannot tie out stops and reports.

## 12 · The agent layer

See `docs/policies.md`. Jobs worth making agentic, with the phase each lands in:
property routing (5) · statement reconciliation (4) · capitalize-vs-expense (2) ·
vendor alias resolution (3) · anomaly detection (6) · close narrative (6).

**Segregation of duties is not achieved and the plan says so plainly** (D-005). The
substitute is quarterly external review. The internal control that works is
distribution monitoring, not anomaly detection — a systematic misclassification is
never an outlier.

Build rules: not a monolith, add jobs behind the existing gate one at a time. Tier
models by what a mistake costs, not by how often the job runs. Keep a golden set.
