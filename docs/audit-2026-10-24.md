# System audit - receipts intake, poller, /api, bank statements and review (2026-10-24)

Five audit agents read the code, the tests, the decision log and the five weeks of fix history; the lead auditor
verified every high-reward claim against the code and pulled the live document store (every receipt and bank-line
card since the 2026-09-21 cutover: 302 receipts, 35 bank cards) to measure the review load instead of guessing at it.
Nothing was changed: no code, no books, no deploy. The five agent reports and the data run are in
`docs/audit-2026-10-24-agents.md`.

## 1. The short answer

**Do not rebuild.** The reads are right, the posting core is sound and tested (578 tests), and the plumbing problems
that filled September (writer misfires, timeouts, replay twins, stale state) have not recurred since 10-02. What is
left is specific and small: a handful of places where two of the three stores (the card store, the Feed tab, the
Journal) stop agreeing and nobody is told; one way a card can post twice; one way a receipt can vanish; no mechanism
between "tests pass" and "it is live"; and a review queue a third of which is already answered.

**What the numbers say about your reviews.** Since cutover, three receipts in four needed no click from you. Of the one
in four you touched, Claude's items were right 93% of the time (you changed the items on 3 of 43 saves). What you
supplied was *who paid* (22 saves) and *which house* (8). Twelve of the 27 "who paid?" cards were one unknown card
number (Mastercard 7952) asked twelve times in one evening. On the bank side, 30 cards are waiting right now and
**12 of them are already settled** (9 answered by receipts you have since saved, 3 leftovers from the first matcher
run), 5 are small bank fees, 5 are partner money that must never be saved as a cost, and 8 are receipts someone still
owes you.

**The ten changes worth making** are in section 3, ranked. The first six are each under a day and need no decision
from you. Section 6 lists the decisions that are yours.

**Four things to be careful of this week** (section 5): the five partner-money bank cards carry an editable purchase
line and the Save button runs no ceiling - Dismiss them with a note, never Save; the first Chase import has a known
bug waiting (one line); the hourly refresh is at 241 of its 360 seconds; the 241 Poinsetta photo from 10-12 is still
in error, never re-read.

## 4. The fix history, and the rebuild question

97 fixes in 33 days since cutover, classified (full ledger in the appendix):

| Root cause | Count | Recurs? |
|---|---|---|
| An outside limit or format (API caps, Netlify limits, Apps Script redirect, iPhone forwards, Drive renditions) | 14 | one per new input shape |
| Something we should have checked and did not (a missing rail) | 17 | yes - three patches on one lookup window |
| Wording for Paul | 15 | yes - raw codes still leak past the lint |
| Self-inflicted: deploy/ops gaps, replays, a fix that broke the next thing | 20 | yes |
| Policy you decided (a D-0xx) | 12 | n/a |
| Stale state believed as truth | 7 | none since 10-02 |
| Performance / timeouts | 6 | none since 10-02 |
| The model read it wrong | 4 | no |
| A field or card number changed | 2 | new card numbers, four times |

Three classes recurred four or more times: **the deploy/ops gap** (push without deploy, six `clasp login` expiries, a
fix unpushed 14 days, a deploy that silently failed every read for two days - #8 fixes the class); **a replay posting a
twin of a migrated row** (five times in nine days, each guarded on the path that bit, ended only because the migration
closed); **stale card state believed as truth** (five consumers each taught separately that the card store is not the
Journal - #2 and #9 are the structural answer). Three fixes caused the next fix (several-receipts-per-email led to the
17-parameter outage; the photo cap led to the 3000 px refusal; the "copy of a waiting card" prompt rule led to six
wrongful holds) - every one a change deployed with no live smoke test.

Firefights per week: 8, 18, 3, 1, 6. The fall after 10-04 is partly real convergence (zero plumbing incidents since
10-02) and partly weeks you barely used it; when you sat down on 10-23 the system produced six defects in a day, but
of a different kind - the first sighting of an input shape (a curly quote in a filename, an iPhone forward) and policy
(refunds, drinks, the ceiling), each now tested.

The prompt grew from 285 to 446 lines in 22 commits; five of its rules are policy that code can enforce (payer
precedent, utility confirmations, tax-share arithmetic, the waiting-card match, the personal-card digits). #1 and #7
move two of them.

**Why not rebuild.** The categories that would argue for it - the model reading wrongly (4 of 97), performance (6,
none since 10-02), state desync (7, none since 10-02) - are small and have stopped. The largest category, outside
limits, is exactly the knowledge a rebuild would have to buy again: the redirect misfire, the 16-nullable-parameter
cap, the 21k streaming cap, the 4 KB env, the 6 MB POST, the 3000 px many-image cap, Drive renditions, iPhone
forwards - every one is now a test or a rail. The one structural smell is three posting paths, and its fix is
deletion (#11), not a rewrite. A full rebuild would discard 578 tests, 92 recorded decisions and a closed migration to
arrive at the same Sheets-plus-model shape you settled on. The right call is the list above: ten changes, most under a
day, that remove the recurring classes at their root.

## 6. Decisions only you can make

1. **Wait for the bank instead of asking.** When a receipt lacks the house or the payer, may it wait up to ten days
   for the bank statement (which names the card, and for Zelle the memo) before it becomes a card? This is the
   largest remaining review reducer after #1 and #7, and it is policy, not code.
2. **Learn card numbers** (#7): may the system write a last-4 to the `Bank accounts` row you pick, or do you want to
   type that tab yourself (D-074 says the numbers change)?
3. **Bank fees** (#16): may a bank fee under, say, $50 post on its own, no card?
4. **Payouts** (#5 step two): one pre-filled card with one Save, or booked by Penny with no card at all? (Constraint 13
   says ask before any close; a payout is after it.)
5. **Label instead of forward** (#10b): will you use a `to-books` Gmail label - one click, or a filter - for the
   vendors that never write to receipts@? One or the other, never both, or the twin rail has to earn its keep.
6. **The ceiling**: 2,000 now. Should a known vendor on a known card clear a higher one, or is 2,000 the number?
7. **Chase deposits**: the first import brings your own deposits in as working capital (D-055). Each asked once, or a
   standing rule "a deposit from Paul into Chase is working capital"?
8. **The two-click Save**: keep "Click again to record $X", or is the totals line enough?
9. Does anyone read properties@'s inbox (its Apps Script failure notices go there), and do you still open the 3 AM
   digest every day (would a missing one be noticed)?
